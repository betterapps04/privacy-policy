Privacy Policy for N4 Kanji Quiz

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the N4 Kanji Quiz mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. We built this App to help learners study JLPT N4 kanji, and we designed it to work entirely on your device, without collecting or transmitting your personal data.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically, the App does not:

  • Require account creation, sign-in, or any personal identifiers
  • Connect to the internet or any external servers
  • Use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location
  • Share any data with third parties

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores your quiz progress (such as which kanji lessons you've completed and your quiz scores) locally on your device using standard device storage. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the App.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App does not collect or transmit any data at all, it may be used by learners of any age.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics providers that collect data.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how the App handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
