JLPT N4 Go

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Short version

JLPT N4 Go does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely offline and keeps all of your study progress on your own device.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. JLPT N4 Go has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no backend server. The app does not request your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

How your data is stored

Your vocabulary progress — including favorites, difficulty flags, review history, and spaced-repetition scheduling — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (AsyncStorage). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not visible to us, the app developer, or any third party
  • Is removed automatically if you uninstall the app

Text-to-speech

The "Listen" and Listening-practice features use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (e.g. Google Text-to-Speech) to read Japanese vocabulary aloud. This processing happens locally on your device — no audio or text is sent to us or to any external server.

Third-party services

JLPT N4 Go does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with third parties because no data is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

JLPT N4 Go does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app is fully offline and account-free, it can be used safely by learners of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional online feature — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
